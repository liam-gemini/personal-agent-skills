# Question Lenses

Select only the lenses that can change the current decision.

## Purpose and scope

- Identify the user, problem, observable success, and explicit non-goals.
- Separate demonstrated requirements from hypothetical future needs.

## Domain and ownership

- Resolve canonical terms, overloaded words, invariants, lifecycle ownership, and authority boundaries.
- Test normal, boundary, concurrent, duplicate, and partial-success scenarios.

## Architecture and data

- Identify the source of truth, consistency requirement, interface, seam, and deployment boundary.
- Expose hidden coupling, irreversible choices, migration constraints, and compatibility obligations.

## Failure and operations

- Examine timeout, retry, idempotency, repair, observability, operator action, and rollback.

## Evolution and cost

- Identify the assumption most likely to change and the cheapest safe validation.
- Account for implementation, maintenance, operations, and cognitive cost.
