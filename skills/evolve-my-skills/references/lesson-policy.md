# Lesson Policy

Promote a lesson when it is cross-project and at least one condition holds:

- the pattern occurred more than once;
- one occurrence had high cost or safety impact;
- the user explicitly identifies it as a stable personal principle;
- it closes a clear procedural gap and is objectively testable.

Reject vague rules such as “be thorough” or “write better code.” Convert them into observable behavior, a trigger, and an exception boundary.

Prefer this form:

> Default behavior. Use an exception only when concrete condition X holds. When taking the exception, state the reason and trade-off.

Keep raw conversation out of formal skills. Preserve only the compressed, reusable lesson.
